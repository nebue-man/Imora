Camera Setup

Three angles, cut live or in post.

Every session records all three feeds separately, so the edit is never locked to one decision made on the day.

Cam 01

Wide master

Locked-off two-shot covering the whole table. The safety angle — always rolling, always usable, and the frame the edit falls back to.

Cam 02

Host close-up

Tighter portrait framing on the host chair, shallow depth of field, set slightly off-axis so eyelines stay natural rather than straight down the barrel.

Cam 03

Guest close-up

Mirrored framing on the guest side. Matched lens and colour profile, so cuts between hosts and guests never jump.
